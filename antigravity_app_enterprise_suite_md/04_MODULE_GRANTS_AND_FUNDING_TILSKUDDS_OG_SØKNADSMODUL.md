# Antigravity Module: Søknad om Tildeling av Midler (Stat, Fylke & Egne Tilskudd)

## 1. Overview & Dual Functionality
Municipalities operate in a dual role regarding grants and subsidies:
1. **Inbound Applications (Kommunen som søker):** Applying for state (*statlige*) and county (*fylkeskommunale*) grants, earmarked funds, and Husbanken subsidies.
2. **Outbound Grant Management (Kommunen som tildeler):** Administering municipal grant schemes for local organizations, culture, sports, integration, and climate initiatives.

This module provides a unified pipeline for both inbound application tracking and outbound grant administration.

---

## 2. Inbound Grants: Municipal Applications to State & County

### Key Funding Categories Covered

#### A. Integration & Settlement (*Integrering, Språk & Bosetting*)
* **Statlige midler (IMDi / KDD):** Integreringstilskudd, norskopplæring og samfunnskunnskap for voksne innvandrere, bosetting av enslige mindreårige flyktninger, og tilskudd til inkludering i lokalsamfunnet.
* **Accounting Mapping:** Mapped to KOSTRA Art 810/800 and Function 271/273. Unused year-end funds automatically earmarked to bound operating funds (*bundne driftsfond*).

#### B. Education & Competence Development (*Utdanning & Kompetanseløft*)
* **Stat/Fylke (Udir / HK-dir / Fylkeskommune):** Kompetanseløftet for spesialpedagogikk og inkludering, svømmeopplæring i barnehager/skoler, yrkesfaglig opplæring, og regionalt kompetansenettverk.

#### C. Care & Accessibility for Persons with Disabilities (*Funksjonshemming & Ressurskrevende Tjenester*)
* **Toppfinansieringsordningen for ressurskrevende helse- og omsorgstjenester (Helsedirektoratet):** Automated calculation of municipal threshold limits (innslagspunkt) and claim compilation for high-cost care users.
* **Husbanken Grants:** Tilskudd til utbedring/tilpassning av boliger, etableringstilskudd, and interest/principal compensation for care-housing investments.

#### D. Regional Development, Climate & Innovation (*Utvikling, Regionalfond & Klima*)
* **Klimasats (Miljødirektoratet) & Bydelsfond/Klimaomstillingsfond:** Innovation grants for zero-emission municipal operations, urban development, and regional business development (SIVA / Forregion).
* **Skjønnstilskudd (Statsforvalteren):** Applications for extraordinary expenses, renewal/innovation projects, and inter-municipal reorganization.

## 3. Outbound Grant Management: Municipal Grant Administration

### Operational Rules & Guidelines
* **Public Visibility & Equal Treatment:** All municipal grant schemes must have politically approved guidelines, clear evaluation criteria, and open public deadlines.
* **Disbursement Rules:**
  - **Grants < 500,000 NOK:** Paid out in full at the start of the budget year upon approval.
  - **Grants >= 500,000 NOK:** Paid out in 4 quarterly installments, subject to annual economic plan revision.
* **Application Lifecycle:**
  1. *Utlysning* (Public Call)
  2. *Søknadsmottak & PII-skjerming*
  3. *AI-assisted Eligibility & Pre-scoring*
  4. *Administrative Recommendation (Kommunedirektørens innstilling)*
  5. *Political Resolution (Bystyre/Hovedutvalg)*
  6. *Svarbrev & Utbetalingsplan*
  7. *Rapportering & Regnskapskontroll*

---

## 4. Earmarked Funds & Accounting Compliance (*Bundne Driftsfond*)
* **Kommuneloven § 14-7 & Budsjett- og regnskapsforskriften:** Earmarked grants received from external sources that are unspent at year-end MUST be transferred to *bundne driftsfond* (art 930 / KOSTRA function 840).
* **Corrected Net Operating Result (*Korrigert netto driftsresultat*):** Automatic adjustment calculations to prevent distorted operating margin reporting due to multi-year grant timing shifts.
